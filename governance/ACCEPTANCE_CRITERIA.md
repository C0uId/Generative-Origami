# ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA

## Parameter-to-Geometry
Evidence includes real geometry or material-allocation changes, directional checks, explicit handling of out-of-domain values, fixed seeds/versions, and reproducible geometry hashes.

## Q4-B01 Physical Base
Evidence includes square paper, no cutting or glue for the first version, identifiable head/body/front-leg/rear-leg/tail material regions, a complete crease pattern, stable IDs, and at least two independent physical refolds.

## Continuous motion
Necessary checks use PASS, FAIL, UNKNOWN/UNCERTAIN, or NOT_APPLICABLE. UNKNOWN/UNCERTAIN remains unresolved. Valid contact is separated from penetration and retains material correspondence, layer-order and signed-gap evidence where applicable.

## Tutorial release
A candidate enters the normal teaching channel only when geometry, complete sequence, path validation, provenance/license and release evidence are all present.
